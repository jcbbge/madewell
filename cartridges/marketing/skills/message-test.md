# Message Test
**Layer:** cartridge (marketing) · **Mode:** quality
**When:** before any claim ships. This is the marketing pack's jig.

---

A jig makes the wrong cut impossible. This one makes an unsourced claim impossible to ship
quietly.

## The pass

For **each claim** in the asset, answer all five. A blank is a stop, not a note.

| # | Question | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What exactly is being claimed? | Cannot state it in one sentence → the asset argues more than one thing. Split it. |
| 2 | What is the source? | No data, no customer words, no demonstrable fact → mark `UNSOURCED`. |
| 3 | Who would dispute this, and what would they say? | Nobody → the claim is too weak to be worth making. |
| 4 | Could a customer or a lawyer puncture it? | Yes → soften to what is true, or source it properly. |
| 5 | Would the audience use these words? | No → rewrite from the captured vocabulary. |

## Output

```markdown
| Claim | Source | Disputed by | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| … | … | … | ship / soften / cut |
```

## The rule

**`UNSOURCED` may not ship.** Cut it, soften it to something true, or go and get the source.
Marking it and shipping anyway is the exact failure this jig exists to prevent — and the mark
makes that failure visible in the record afterwards, which is the point.
