# Discovery Lenses — Marketing

**Extends:** the kernel's `discovery` skill (`.madewell/skills/discovery.md`), via its Lens Slot.
**When:** loaded with the marketing cartridge; run *after* the universal core on every pass.

The engine — classification, pass modes, routing, The One Thing — lives in the kernel and does
not change here. This adds what a marketing lane can see that the universal core cannot name.

---

## Lens M1 — Language

The gap between their words and ours.

- Verbatim phrases for the problem. Quote, never paraphrase — the paraphrase is the loss.
- Words they *avoid*. If nobody says "platform", that is a finding about our copy.
- Metaphors they reach for. A metaphor is a ready-made campaign.
- Where they correct the interviewer's vocabulary. That correction is the headline.

## Lens M2 — Objection

What they push back on, and what they already dismissed.

- Objections stated outright.
- Objections implied by a hesitation, a hedge, or a change of subject.
- Alternatives they already rejected, and why — that reasoning is reusable.
- What they think this costs, before being told. The gap is a pricing signal.

## Lens M3 — Trigger

Why now.

- The event that started the search. Not the pain — the pain was there last year.
- Who noticed first, and what made it their problem.
- What deadline or consequence is attached.
- Absence of a trigger is itself a finding: this is interesting, not urgent.

## Lens M4 — Proof

What evidence would actually move them.

- What they asked to see.
- Who they said they would check with.
- What they would consider disqualifying.
- Whether they trust numbers, peers, or a demonstration — these are not interchangeable.

## Lens M5 — Channel fit

Where this person already is.

- Where they said they heard about things.
- What they read, watch, or attend — named, not assumed.
- Who they consider a credible voice in the space.
