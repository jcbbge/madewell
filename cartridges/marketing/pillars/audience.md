# Pillar — Audience

*Who they are and what they believe now.*

## Principles

1. **A persona is a claim about reality and can be wrong.** Treat it as falsifiable, not as
   set dressing.
2. **What they believe now matters more than who they are.** Demographics rarely predict
   behaviour; current belief does.
3. **The trigger is the unit of timing.** Nobody buys because they match a profile; they buy
   because something happened.
4. **Their words, not ours.** The vocabulary gap between how a company describes a problem
   and how a customer describes it is where most marketing fails silently.

## Protocol

1. Name one real person you could email. If you cannot, stop; that is the finding.
2. Write what they believe **today** about the problem — including the wrong parts.
3. Write what they would have to believe to act.
4. Name the **trigger**: the event that makes this urgent rather than interesting.
5. Collect their **actual words** — from calls, tickets, reviews, forums. Quote, do not
   paraphrase.
6. Name who this is **not** for, and why.

## Definition of done

- A named, reachable person or a direct quotation from one.
- Belief-now and belief-required are both written, and they differ.
- A trigger is named that is an event, not a state.
- At least five verbatim customer phrases are captured and available to the message pillar.
