# Pillar — Channel

*Where it goes, and what that medium demands.*

## Principles

1. **Message before channel.** Choosing the container before the content is how a
   thirty-second idea becomes a whitepaper nobody finishes.
2. **The medium has requirements and they are not negotiable.** Length, pace, the first three
   seconds, whether sound is on. Fighting them loses.
3. **Be where they already are.** A channel you must teach someone to visit is a product
   launch, not a channel.
4. **One channel done properly beats five done adequately.** Adequate is invisible.

## Protocol

1. State the message and its length in its natural form. How long does the idea actually
   need?
2. List the channels where the named audience already is — evidence, not assumption.
3. For each, write what the medium demands.
4. Pick the one where natural length and medium demand overlap best. Say why the others lose.
5. Adapt form without changing the claim. **If the claim has to change to fit, the channel is
   wrong.**

## Definition of done

- The channel is chosen after the message, and the order is visible in the record.
- Evidence exists that the audience is there.
- The claim survived the adaptation intact.
- The rejected channels are named, with reasons.
