# Pillar — Discovery

*What is broken, for whom, costing what.*

## Principles

1. **Their words, verbatim.** The moment you paraphrase their problem into your category, you
   have started selling and stopped learning.
2. **Quantify or mark it.** A cost with no number is a story. Stories do not survive
   procurement.
3. **Ask what they already tried.** Failed attempts tell you the real constraint, and what not
   to propose.
4. **Silence is data.** What they will not discuss is usually the actual blocker.

## Protocol

1. Ask what happens today, step by step. Do not interrupt with a solution.
2. Capture the problem **in their words**. Quote it into the piece file.
3. Quantify: how often, how long, how many people, how much.
4. Ask what they have already tried and why it did not work.
5. Ask who else this affects — that is the map of the buying group.
6. Ask what happens if this is still true in a year. The answer separates urgent from
   interesting.
7. Book the next step **in the room**, with a date.

## Definition of done

- The problem is recorded in the customer's own words, quoted.
- A cost figure, or `UNQUANTIFIED` and a plan to get it.
- Prior attempts are known.
- Everyone affected is named.
- A next step exists with a date on it.
