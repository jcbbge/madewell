# Pillar — Close

*The decision path, and what blocks it.*

## Principles

1. **You cannot close a process you have not mapped.** "Waiting to hear back" means the map
   is missing.
2. **The champion is not the decider** — usually. Find out which, explicitly, rather than
   hoping.
3. **Surface the blocker early.** Legal, security, procurement, a competing project. Each
   costs weeks and none improves with age.
4. **A stalled deal is information.** Slipping it forward a quarter is not a forecast, it is
   an avoidance.

## Protocol

1. Ask directly: what are the steps between yes and signature?
2. Name every person who can say no. Including the one who says nothing.
3. Ask what has stopped a purchase like this before, here.
4. Get legal and security started **before** verbal agreement, not after.
5. Confirm the budget exists and whose it is.
6. Agree the signature date with them, out loud.
7. If it stalls: ask what changed. Record the answer. Re-qualify or close it lost — and
   losing it cleanly is a legitimate, valuable outcome.

## Definition of done

- The steps from yes to signature are written down.
- Every person who can say no is named.
- Known blockers are in motion, not queued.
- A signature date exists that the customer said out loud.
