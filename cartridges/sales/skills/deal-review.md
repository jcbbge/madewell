# Deal Review
**Layer:** cartridge (sales) · **Mode:** quality
**When:** before forecasting, and before any proposal is sent. This is the sales pack's jig.

---

Adversarial by construction. The question is not "how is this deal going" — everyone answers
that optimistically. The question is **what kills it**.

## The pass

| # | Question | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What breaks if they do nothing? | No answer → not qualified. Say so out loud. |
| 2 | What does the status quo cost, per month? | `UNQUANTIFIED` → no urgency exists; get the number or expect a slip. |
| 3 | Who signs? | Cannot name a person → the deal is not forecastable at any percentage. |
| 4 | What are the steps between yes and signature? | Unknown → you are guessing at the close date. |
| 5 | Who can say no, and have we met them? | An unmet blocker → the most likely cause of loss. |
| 6 | What has stopped a purchase like this here before? | Never asked → ask before proposing. |
| 7 | If we lose, what will the reason turn out to have been? | No answer → you do not understand the deal yet. |
| 8 | Will they be glad a year from now? | No → the rubric fails. Walk away and say why. |

## Output

```markdown
| Question | Answer | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| … | … | high / medium / low |

**Most likely cause of loss:** …
**Verdict:** forecast / re-qualify / disqualify
```

## The rule

**A deal with no named signer and no quantified cost does not enter the forecast.** It may
still be worked — plenty of real deals start there — but it is not a number anyone should be
planning against, and calling it one is how a quarter is lost quietly.
