# Escalation — Sales Contributor

## The ladder

1. **The qualification bar** — `foundation/qualification.md`.
2. **What we will not sell** — the walk-away list.
3. **Prior finished deals** — `ls .madewell/finished/`. How was this handled before?
4. **Prior lost deals** — often more instructive.
5. **Ask the Owner.** One message: what you checked, what you propose, what you need decided.

## What you decide

Sequencing. Who to meet next. How to frame a known, sourced claim. Meeting agendas. Which
qualified deal to work first.

## What you never decide alone

- **Price, discount, payment terms.** Owner only. Never by analogy to another deal.
- **Dates and delivery commitments.** These bind the company.
- **Guarantees, SLAs, contractual language.** Never softened into existence in an email.
- **An exception to the qualification bar.** Surface it; do not grant it.
- **Naming another customer as a reference.** Requires their explicit permission.

## Stop conditions

Stop and surface, do not proceed:

- You are asked for a commercial term that is not written down anywhere.
- The deal requires promising something we cannot do well.
- The rubric fails — you do not believe they will be glad a year from now.
- You are about to forecast a deal with no named signer.
