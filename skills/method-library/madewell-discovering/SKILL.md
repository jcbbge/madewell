# Discovery

Shape incoming material into coherent possible work without losing the outcome sought or confusing a candidate with permission to act. Rough notes, transcripts, fragments and existing work are valid inputs; a finished specification is not an entrance requirement.

This is an unpublished method proposal, not an accepted shipped default. This entry selects methods; it does not duplicate their full instructions or dispatch agents.

## Select for the contribution needed

The default sequence is **Declare → lookFirst → classify → Lenses → oneThing → route → confirm**:

- **Declare** — declaring intent: establish sourced outcomes, scope and explicit versus interpreted intent.
- **lookFirst** — looking first: inspect existing material and capabilities before replacement proposals.
- **classify** — classifying: distinguish requests, observations, constraints, alternatives and assumptions; account for intent.
- **Lenses** — applying lenses: examine grounded DX/UX/AX consequences and choices.
- **oneThing** — shaping candidates: retain coherent outcome candidates alongside necessary preparation.
- **route** — routing: identify genuine prerequisites, question owners and independent work.
- **confirm** — confirming: reconcile coverage and preserve the accepted identities and dependency graph.

These descriptive selections refer to separately available methods, not sibling-file reads. Obtain selected instructions through the supported host path; catalog names alone do not establish loading or application. If an explicit selection is unavailable, report it rather than silently substituting. A method can be used alone, repeated or replaced compatibly. Do not require seven agents, calls, documents, or a predetermined number of tasks. Do not rerun supplied valid contributions merely to simulate a complete sequence.

## Carry the material, not just the summary

Receive exact source, authorized prior contributions, settled decisions, existing work and relevant saved answers. Preserve source identities and locators separately; distinguish what a source says from what inspection establishes or interpretation proposes. Retain intent, requirement, disposition, candidate, artifact, condition, question and dependency identities across contributions. New facts or clarified intent must retain their provenance and original anchors rather than quietly rewriting earlier records.

Every intent needs linked requirements or an explicit sourced disposition (provided, context, deferred, superseded). Every requirement needs candidate ownership. Preserve contradictory inputs and unknowns honestly. A research inventory may be a useful prerequisite but does not deliver the requested capability. Each selected task retains its own Delivery; do not make four phase-shaped candidates to imitate Delivery's diagram.

Ground claims in actual available material; account for applicable Rumen fences, independent judgment, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners. Acknowledging rules or retaining hashes is not proof of executed checks. Prepare only configured successors with actual contributions and resolvable permitted inputs; keep private tracks private. Cross-task preparation promises, recipient handoffs and environment setup are different things.

For agent exchanges, state understanding and use NQ: reuse scoped answers, ask concrete missing/ambiguous inputs only, preserve answered turns, and after three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. Clear input uses zero question rounds. Keep contributions and exchanges in the existing work record before a successor proceeds when possible; disclose absent persistence. Recover saved results before retrying uncertain actions.

## Effort and return

Light is the free default: preserve all explicit intent and material constraints, coherent outcome candidates, honest questions and real dependencies using available material. Focused additionally investigates a consequential gap or competing interpretation and reconciles its effect on candidates. Deep additionally traces difficult outcome/constraint/dependency relationships into accessible primary material and walks concrete cases through the decomposition, revising it when justified. Return the cases/observations actually used, not a claim that a separate behavioral evaluation passed.

Distinguish requested, authorized/effective and performed effort; identify actual sources, added investigation and limits. Standalone public use is unrestricted by managed commerce. A managed service authorizes its paid Focused/Deep operations separately; unavailable access or capability requires disclosure and an explicit alternative, not silent upgrade/downgrade. Every level retains correctness, safety, privacy, required evidence and recovery. Effort does not choose a provider or grant work authority.

Return natural Markdown with source-linked intent/coverage, candidate purposes/boundaries/conditions, artifacts, dependencies/reasons, question owners and unresolved inputs. Stop when this is usable possible work or a specific gap prevents further faithful shaping. Confirming is not user selection. Selection must explicitly identify candidates and show prerequisite consequences; it neither selects unchosen prerequisites nor dispatches execution.

**Example:** “We need automated invoice reminders” may yield an outcome candidate plus a prerequisite consent-policy decision. Do not replace the reminder capability with “research reminders,” select either automatically, or send reminders during discovery.
