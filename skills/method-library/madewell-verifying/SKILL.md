# Verification

Verify the actual result against the current contract. Neither a maker's “done,” valid JSON, a matching hash, a different profile label nor a successful process alone establishes the intended outcome.

## Apply

Receive the actual candidate/result and version, current task/conditions, fixed brief, requirements and permitted source/environment, declared evidence owners and independently provisioned inputs. Preserve intent, requirement/disposition, artifact, condition, dependency and question identities. Used alone, inspect the supplied result and conditions without claiming predecessor work occurred or requiring sibling packages.

1. Establish your real authority and independence from making and acceptance-check design. The maker does not award independent acceptance; changing labels does not make self-review independent. If your actual role is only source review or self-inspection, report that boundary instead of using an independent acceptance verdict.
2. Bind each claim to the actual result/version and current condition. Determine what evidence each condition requires and which producer can establish it. Keep source identities distinct from locators, observed results distinct from attributed assertions, and historical acceptance distinct from current applicability.
3. Obtain every applicable condition's required evidence at the necessary rigor, or record failure/unknown. A candidate checker can use the candidate, fixed plan and actually provisioned environment after implementation and before verification. It cannot require its own future report, inaccessible private maker material, future release or unperformed manual QA as its inputs. Live observations, operator judgments and engine provenance need their own authorized evidence producers and timing.
4. Under software's independent split, only the designated non-maker executor combines the actual candidate and independent checks. Check design derives from the same fixed plan, blind to the maker; the maker must not see private check-design output. If execution belongs to another owner, obtain their attributable results or mark not run—this skill does not transfer that authority. Non-software verification uses domain-appropriate evidence, not mandatory Git, test commands or deployment.
5. Record actual operations/observations, executor, inputs/environment, result version, condition links, outcomes and limits. Account for applicable Rumen mechanical fences, independent judgment, operator-owned guidance and vocabulary using their actual definitions/evidence owners. Acknowledging a rule or hashing bytes is not a passed check. Missing and unavailable evidence stay distinct from observed failure; neither can become pass.
6. Report an attributable pass only within the conditions and authority actually supported. Retain failures and unknowns without changing the condition to fit the result. A check pass is not proof of live usability when live observation is required, nor is source publication proof of installed/native activation.

Do not silently fix the artifact, rewrite checks, relax acceptance, select tasks, publish, contact people, spend money or deploy. Return a failure with evidence to its configured responsible owner; distinguish an observed symptom from a cause not yet established. Plan/implementation, check design, an external prerequisite and unavailable evidence may need different next actions. A prerequisite passing does not prove this consumer passes; name its resumption check.

Every agent exchange uses NQ: state understanding, reuse scoped answers, ask concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs only, preserve question identity/source/owner/blocked role and answered-turn count. After three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. A clear brief needs no ritual questions. Keep private tracks private and retain results/exchanges in the existing record before successors proceed when supported; disclose absent persistence. Recover saved results/receipts before retrying uncertain actions; do not repeat an effect just because its response was lost.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** obtain the required evidence for **every applicable condition at its necessary rigor**, or report failure/unknown with exact gaps. No required safety, accessibility, recovery or live proof is optional on Light.
- **Focused:** additionally perform authorized adverse-case coverage beyond that required baseline, retaining the actual cases, results and condition relationships.
- **Deep:** additionally perform justified, authorized interaction, failure/recovery, stress or independent reproduction work beyond baseline, retaining exact inputs and observations and revisiting the verdict where warranted.

When a condition already requires difficult or extensive work, it belongs to baseline; do not relabel it premium or claim it was performed without evidence. Record requested, authorized/effective and performed effort separately. Public standalone use is unrestricted by managed commerce; managed paid Focused/Deep access is service-authorized. Missing access/capability needs disclosure and an explicit alternative, never silent substitution. Effort changes neither provider nor work authority and never weakens correctness, safety, privacy, evidence or recovery.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown: result/version, each condition with passed/failed/unknown evidence status and attributable observations, actual checks performed/not run, limitations, actual effort, and next action/owner for every gap. Retain usable permitted evidence for each selected consumer, not inaccessible references or a generic reassurance. Stop when all current conditions have an honest evidence account or a precise blocked observation prevents one. A standalone verification result need not invent a successor or a release action.

**Example:** An approved brochure PDF passes text/brand review, but print legibility is a current condition and no printed proof is available. Report that condition unknown and its next evidence owner; do not accept the brochure as print-ready or weaken the condition because Light was selected.

Unpublished method proposal; source output is not behavioral or native-host acceptance.
