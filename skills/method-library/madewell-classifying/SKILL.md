# classify

Keep what is wanted, observed, constrained, proposed and assumed distinct. A useful reformulation must not erase an intent or promote a guess into a requirement.

## Apply

Receive exact source, available intent/observation contributions, settled decisions and scoped answers. This method can run alone: identify source statements locally when prior records were not supplied, without claiming earlier methods ran. Preserve existing identities when they are supplied.

1. Classify statements as requested outcomes, observations, constraints, alternatives, assumptions or questions. Retain contradictory statements and their authors/sources. An observation is not automatically requested work; a proposed means is not automatically a constraint.
2. Give each derived requirement an identity, outcome/constraint kind, text, source links, **explicit** or **interpretation** origin, and the intent identities it accounts for. Source identity and actual locator/version remain distinct; valid links do not prove the requirement is true or authorized.
3. Account for every declared intent through requirements or a sourced **disposition**: provided, context, deferred or superseded. Retain its intent identity, reason and source. “Provided” requires support that the outcome is already supplied; “superseded” requires the replacing decision/source. Do not use either as a convenient dismissal. Preserve a disposition's supplied identity when one exists; otherwise keep it addressable by intent and contribution.
4. Do not both dispose of an intent and assign it to requirements as if nothing changed. If classification reveals a new outcome or changed interpretation, retain the original anchor and record the sourced change rather than silently rewriting the prior contribution. An unresolved contradiction remains unresolved.
5. Carry questions by identity with source, answer owner and blocking role or none. Reuse valid scoped answers; distinguish an unanswered fact from a delegated choice that the receiving owner should settle.

Ground classification in actual material. Carry applicable Rumen fences, independent judgment, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners. Rule acknowledgments, source hashes and tidy categories are not proof of compliance, executed checks or independent acceptance. No classification grants execution, publication, spending, extra access or task selection.

Use NQ for each agent exchange: state understanding, ask only concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs, preserve answers and answered-turn count. A clear brief needs no question rounds; after three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert the responsible human. Preserve private-track boundaries. Retain the contribution in the existing record before handoff when possible; disclose missing persistence and recover saved results before retrying interrupted work.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** classify material statements and account for every supplied intent with requirements or explicit sourced dispositions.
- **Focused:** additionally inspect a consequential inference/contradiction against supplied decisions or examples; expose what changes in requirement wording or origin.
- **Deep:** additionally trace difficult overlapping intent/constraint relationships to primary material and concrete counterexamples; revise the mapping or retain the exact unresolved conflict.

Keep requested, authorized/effective and performed effort separate, citing actual investigation and limits. Public standalone use is unrestricted by managed commerce. Managed paid Focused/Deep authorization comes from the service; missing access/capability needs disclosure and an explicit alternative, not silent substitution. No tier weakens correctness, safety, privacy, required evidence or recovery, changes provider settings, or expands authority.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown with categorized statements, identified requirements and intent links, sourced dispositions, contradictions and question owners, plus actual effort. Prepare the next selected consumer with exact authorized sources and this mapping; no sibling method or invented successor is required. Stop when all input intent is visibly accounted for or a precise source gap prevents faithful classification. Candidate ownership and implementation design belong to later selected work.

**Example:** “Managers need a weekly summary; a spreadsheet might work” yields an explicit summary outcome and a spreadsheet alternative, not a spreadsheet requirement. “No personal identifiers” is a constraint. Neither statement vanishes because the alternative is rejected.
