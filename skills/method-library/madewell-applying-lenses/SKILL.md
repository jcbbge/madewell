# Lenses

Judge a choice in its actual context and purpose before prescribing it. Relevant perspectives come from the material, not from an assumed software stack or a generic scoring worksheet.

## Apply

Receive the intended outcome, exact source, requirements/constraints, observations, settled decisions and questions. Used alone, identify these from supplied material without claiming earlier methods ran. Keep original identities and explicit versus interpreted origin; do not silently turn a preference or risk into a requirement.

For each material choice, apply the supplied rubric. The Made Well default gives equal standing to:

- **DX:** developing, understanding, inspecting, changing and recovering the work. This is not merely the agent's convenience.
- **UX:** usefulness, confidence, cognitive load, recovery, craft and a memorable/lovable experience for the person served.
- **AX:** clear capabilities and boundaries, discoverable correct action, inspectable state, verifiable results and reduced ambiguity/wasted context.

Identify concrete gains, costs, risks, affected perspectives, uncertainty and longer-term consequences. Use measurements only when observed; “toward 10×” is ambition, not a claimed multiplier. Account for resource, latency, money, token and human-attention effects within these experiences. Do not invent weights or average away a serious weakness.

The default seeks improvement in at least two experiences, explicitly justifying a cost to the third: three hits build with conviction; two proceed with the sacrifice explained; fewer reshape or stop. Retain any actually supplied matrix definitions/cells, but do not fabricate cell numbers for absent matrices. A selected rubric does not override hard constraints or become immutable grammar.

Give tradeoffs and decisions distinguishable identities linked to the affected requirement identities and actual source identities/locators. Record choice, rationale, responsible owner and the observation that could change it. Retain prior decisions unless new evidence justifies an explicit revision. Predictions are not proof of measured benefit. Resolve delegated choices; retrieve missing facts. Ask only when a missing consequential fact/preference or reserved authority cannot be responsibly inspected, settled, deferred or contained.

Ground claims in available material. Carry applicable mechanical fences, independent judgment, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners. Acknowledgments, hashes and model agreement do not establish a passed check or independent acceptance. This method authorizes no implementation, task selection, spending, publication or additional data access.

For agent exchanges, confirm understanding and use NQ, reusing scoped answers rather than manufacturing rounds. Preserve question identity/source, answer owner, blocked role, answers and count; after three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. Keep private-track boundaries. Retain results before a successor proceeds in the existing record when available; disclose absent persistence and recover saved contributions before retrying interrupted work.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** examine the actual consequential choice through all three experiences, retaining concrete costs, uncertainty and rationale.
- **Focused:** additionally compare a materially different feasible alternative and an adverse case against the same requirements; show what changed the choice.
- **Deep:** additionally investigate the decisive assumption in accessible primary material or an authorized discriminating observation, then revisit the choice and its recovery consequences.

Distinguish requested, authorized/effective and performed effort with actual observations and limits. Standalone public use is unrestricted by managed commerce; managed paid Focused/Deep authorization is external. Disclose unavailable access/capability and offer an explicit alternative without silent substitution. Required correctness, safety, privacy, evidence and recovery apply at every level; effort does not select a provider or expand authority.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown: sourced tradeoffs, affected requirements, decision/owner/rationale, uncertainty and evidence that would change it, actual effort, and the next selected consumer's usable permitted material. A standalone result needs no invented successor. Stop when the choice is justified or a precise unresolved fact blocks it; do not reopen settled goals merely to make alternatives interesting.

**Example:** A shorter consent form may reduce reader burden but conceal the scope of data use. Name that UX risk and AX ambiguity rather than awarding “simplicity” a high score. Retain the consent constraint while comparing a clearer concise version.
