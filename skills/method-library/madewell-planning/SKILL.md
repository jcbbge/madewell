# Planning

Make the chosen approach executable within its actual authority, with proof that its evidence owner can genuinely obtain. A compact brief is enough when it settles the work; a large PRD is not mandatory.

## Apply

Receive one selected task, grounded approach, exact source, requirements/constraints, settled decisions/answers, artifact/condition identities, dependencies and unfinished questions. Inspect the relevant real environment, available capabilities, existing patterns and consumers before specifying changes. Used alone, use the supplied approach/task directly; do not invent predecessor work or require sibling methods.

Produce the smallest actionable fixed brief that supplies:

- **Outcome and scope:** intended result, exact deliverables/locations or action targets, exclusions, authority, source/requirement coverage and invariants. Preserve original identities and explicit-versus-interpreted provenance. Name existing examples/patterns to follow when they materially constrain the work.
- **Actions and inputs:** settled implementation choices, feasible ordered actions, authorized resources, dependencies/owners and real readiness evidence. Preserve independently owed early prerequisite artifacts rather than making them wait for their consumer's final completion. No hidden choice that would force the implementer to redesign the task.
- **Conditions and proof:** each stable condition identity and observable claim, actual artifact/version it concerns, evidence producer/owner, specific accessible inputs, availability boundary, and limits. Required judgment, safety, accessibility, recovery or live evidence does not disappear because it is expensive.
- **Contingencies:** what stops the affected operation, question/answer owner, permitted recovery, uncertain-effect handling, preservation of partials and the exact resumption action. Settle ordinary choices using context, purpose and the supplied DX/UX/AX rubric; retain concrete gains/costs and uncertainty, not invented scores. Genuine unresolvable inputs remain explicit blockers, not a falsely ready brief.
- **Successor preparation:** the actual brief, resolvable permitted source/material and relevant decisions/answers for every selected consumer. A terminal planning result needs no invented agent or successor.

## Make evidence available at the right boundary

A candidate checker may use the candidate, plan and provisioned environment available after implementation and before verification. Do not assign it its own future report, future release, an operator verdict not yet made, or inaccessible private maker material as proof. Engine provenance, manual judgment and live checks need their own actual authorized producers and timing. If a necessary input cannot be provisioned, expose the gap; do not weaken or circularly redefine the condition.

For software's independent split, provision implementation and acceptance-check design from the same fixed plan without exposing their private making to each other. The authorized non-maker executor later combines checks and candidate. Distinguish any plan-required maintenance of existing regression tests from independent acceptance-check authorship. Makers do not run those checks or award acceptance. No code, Git, test command or deployment is mandatory for a non-software task.

Ground sources by actual locator/version and findings, keeping identity distinct from location. Account for applicable Rumen mechanical fences, independent judgment, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners; an acknowledgment or fingerprint does not prove a gate ran. Skill loading and effort grant neither execution nor broader access, publication, contact or spending.

Use NQ for agent exchanges: state understanding, reuse scoped answers, preserve question identities/owners/blocked roles and answered turns. Ask concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs only; after three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. Preserve private tracks and save the brief/exchange in the existing record before successors proceed when possible, disclosing absent persistence. Recover recorded boundaries before retrying uncertain operations.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** settle the smallest feasible fixed brief with all scope, inputs, conditions, proof owners/availability and recovery obligations.
- **Focused:** additionally inspect affected consumers and a material sequencing/contingency seam; show how actual findings change or support the brief.
- **Deep:** additionally resolve difficult dependencies using accessible primary material and concrete failure/recovery examples before freezing the brief. Missing inputs remain owned gaps rather than fictional resolution.

Declare any additional implementation checkpoints or difficult transformation/recovery work here, before making; an effort upgrade during implementation cannot add them. Report requested, authorized/effective and performed effort with actual investigation and limits. Standalone public use is unrestricted by managed commerce; managed paid Focused/Deep access is service-authorized. Disclose unavailable access/capability and offer an explicit alternative, not silent substitution. Every level retains correctness, safety, privacy, required evidence and recovery; effort chooses no provider and expands no authority.

## Return and boundary

Return the fixed brief in natural Markdown with its source/decision basis, proof provisioning and any blocking question. Stop when recipients can perform their actual roles without redesign, or name the precise defect preventing readiness. Do not carry out implementation or claim planned checks passed. Revisions are explicit and preserve historical attempts; changed material makes affected preparation stale.

**Example:** For a translation deliverable, give the independent reviewer the actual translation, approved glossary and source text. “Prove the reviewer later approved it” is not a candidate-time condition the same reviewer can use as an input to its own review.

Unpublished method proposal; source output is not behavioral or native-host acceptance.
