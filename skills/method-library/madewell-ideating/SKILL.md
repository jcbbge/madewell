# Ideation

Shape how to fulfill this task, not a new task you would rather do. Reuse settled decisions and answers; open-ended Brainstorm and task-specific Ideation have different boundaries.

## Apply

Receive the selected task's identity, intended outcome and exact source, requirements, constraints, artifacts/conditions, dependencies, existing material, prior choices and questions. Rough input is permitted; recover accessible context before asking the person to supply a finished specification. Used alone, identify the actual task from the assignment without fabricating Discovery outputs or requiring sibling methods.

1. Explain what success means for the person served, which constraints/invariants matter, and what is explicit versus interpreted. Preserve intent, requirement/disposition, artifact, condition, dependency and question identities. A necessary preparatory result does not replace the requested capability.
2. Inspect relevant actual material and existing capabilities. Give sources distinct identities and exact locators/versions, with findings and limits. Ground the approach in what those findings establish rather than treating a file list or hash as understanding.
3. Develop a feasible approach within this task. Select a thinking specialist only for an actual obstruction and only when its instructions are available; do not automatically load a toolbox or invent agents. Retain a sound conventional answer rather than manufacturing novelty.
4. Settle delegated material choices using the supplied rubric. By default give equal standing to DX (understanding/change/recovery), UX (usefulness, confidence, craft/cognitive load) and AX (clear capabilities, inspectability, verifiability/ambiguity). State concrete gains/costs, rationale and uncertainty; do not invent weights or measured multipliers. The default seeks at least two improvements with any third-experience cost explicit; fewer calls or a shorter answer alone prove no gain.
5. Address consequential uncertainty through accessible facts or an authorized discriminating observation. Preserve unresolved prerequisites and questions with source, owner and blocked role. Reuse valid scoped answers. Do not demand resolution of an uncertainty before it changes the justified next action, or pretend a decision made an unknown fact true.

Carry applicable Rumen fences, independent judgment, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners. Predictions and maker statements are not executed checks or independent acceptance. No approach choice grants implementation, spending, publication, contact, extra data access or task selection. A plan defect discovered later returns for explicit revision, not silent redesign while making.

Every agent exchange uses NQ: state understanding, ask only concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs, reuse answers and preserve count. After three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. Keep private tracks private and retain the contribution before handoff in the existing record when supported; disclose absent durability. Recover saved results and uncertain-effect evidence before retrying interrupted actions.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** ground success and select a suitable approach, naming its main consequential uncertainty and required inputs.
- **Focused:** additionally compare a material feasible alternative and a serious adverse case against the same outcome/constraints; show why the choice holds or changes.
- **Deep:** additionally investigate the decisive assumption in accessible primary evidence or an explicitly authorized discriminating artifact, then revisit the approach and its failure boundary.

Record requested, authorized/effective and performed effort with actual sources, comparisons, artifact work and limits. Public standalone use is unrestricted by managed commerce; managed paid Focused/Deep access is authorized externally. Missing access/capability needs disclosure and an explicit alternative, never silent substitution. Every level retains correctness, safety, privacy, required evidence and recovery; effort neither selects a provider nor expands authority.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown: success account, chosen approach and rationale, relevant evidence, preserved decisions, unresolved questions/dependencies/owners, actual effort and useful inputs for the next selected consumer. Include only permitted material, not private sibling work. A standalone result need not invent a successor. Stop once the approach is grounded enough to plan, or identify the precise input preventing that. Do not execute the approach or call it verified.

**Example:** The selected task is a clearer refund-policy page. Ideation may choose a question-led structure based on actual support questions; it must not replace the page with “research customer support” or reopen the already settled refund policy.

Unpublished method proposal; source output is not behavioral or native-host acceptance.
