# route

Show what truly needs what, which work is independent, and who owes unresolved inputs. Topical similarity and a preparation note are not dependency edges.

## Apply

Receive candidate identities, their requirements, artifact producers and conditions, exact source, accepted questions/answers and existing relationships. Used alone, work from the supplied candidates without inventing prior phases. Keep existing identities and explicit-versus-interpreted provenance.

1. For each prerequisite, identify a stable dependency identity, producer, consumer, reason, source identities/actual locators, required artifact identity when applicable, and the completion evidence needed for readiness. The artifact must belong to its stated producer. Distinguish an external prerequisite/owner from another known candidate; do not invent a candidate identity for unavailable external work.
2. Retain only genuine ordering: the consumer cannot fulfill its obligation without the producer result. Remove no accepted edge silently. Record independent branches and shared prerequisites; parallel possibility is not a promise to launch concurrent workers.
3. Inspect cycles, self-dependencies and preparations that wait for their own consumer. If an early independently useful artifact is needed, propose an explicit candidate revision/split to its owner, retaining original outcome anchors. Do not silently rekey candidates or expand scope.
4. Give cross-task preparation promises stable identities: which producer supplies what usable result to which consumer, why and from what source. Required ordering must also appear in the dependency graph. A promise alone does not schedule work or prove the prerequisite ready.
5. Preserve question identities, source, answer owners, blocking roles and candidate bindings. A task that produces an uncertainty inventory does not thereby resolve those uncertainties for the downstream outcome. Carry the remaining obligation and its owner explicitly. Reuse valid scoped answers rather than asking again.

The inspected delivery contract supports **delivered-task readiness**, not release of a consumer halfway through its producer task. Do not promise within-task parallel release that the selected host cannot represent. Keep conceptual dependencies distinct from actual supported execution; no runtime is required to return this analysis. Environment setup and recipient-specific handoff preparation are not cross-task dependency promises.

Ground routing in available material; distinguish findings from assumptions and unknown readiness. Carry applicable Rumen fences, independent judgment, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners. A source hash, preparation acknowledgment or completed producer claim is not automatically readiness proof, and prerequisite success does not prove consumer acceptance. This method grants no selection, dispatch, implementation, spending, publication or extra data access.

Use NQ for agent exchanges: state understanding, reuse scoped answers, preserve question/answer provenance and answered-turn count, and ask concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs only. After three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. Keep private tracks private; save contributions before handoff in the existing record when supported, disclosing absent persistence. Recover saved dependency/effect evidence before retrying interrupted work.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** retain genuine edges/reasons, prerequisite artifacts, question owners and independent branches, with honest readiness gaps.
- **Focused:** additionally inspect a shared prerequisite or disputed edge against the actual producer/consumer contracts; reconcile promises with the graph.
- **Deep:** additionally trace a completion-critical dependency chain through accessible primary artifacts and failure/recovery cases; revise unsupported readiness assumptions explicitly.

Distinguish requested, authorized/effective and performed effort with actual inspected relationships and limits. Standalone public use is unrestricted by managed commerce; paid managed Focused/Deep authorization is external. Missing access/capability requires disclosure and an explicit alternative, not silent substitution. Every tier retains required correctness, safety, privacy, evidence and recovery; effort changes neither provider nor authority.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown: dependencies with identities/reasons/artifact producers/readiness evidence; preparation promises; independent branches; external prerequisites and questions/owners; actual effort. Supply the next selected consumer your actual routing records and resolvable permitted material, not a reconstructed final graph. Stop when ordering and remaining readiness gaps are usable or a precise missing input prevents them. Do not select prerequisites on anyone's behalf.

**Example:** Both a printed guide and a support page need approved policy A-policy. They depend on that delivered policy, not on each other merely because they share a topic. Keep the two consumers independent and preserve the policy approval evidence requirement.

Unpublished method proposal; source output is not behavioral or native-host acceptance.
