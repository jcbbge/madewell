# oneThing

Find coherent units of desired outcome, not a predetermined number of tasks. Necessary preparation must not silently replace the result the person actually wants.

## Apply

Receive exact source, intent/requirements/dispositions, existing-work observations, tradeoffs, settled decisions and scoped questions/answers. This method works alone from supplied material; identify local anchors without fabricating a completed discovery sequence. Preserve existing identities whenever present.

1. Reconcile existing work before proposing duplicates. Group work by a coherent purpose and observable result with clear boundaries. Do not force unrelated outcomes together merely to reduce task count or split phases merely to increase it.
2. Give each candidate a stable identity, title, purpose/outcome, scope/exclusions, requirement and intent coverage, source links, and identifiable observable conditions. Keep every required outcome and constraint owned by at least one candidate; explain shared ownership without conflicting writes or competing deliverables.
3. Retain an explicit outcome candidate for each sought capability/result, including blocked or unapproved outcomes, unless an explicit sourced disposition accounts for it. An uncertainty inventory or research artifact cannot by itself deliver a capability. Preserve the outcome while making its prerequisites visible.
4. Identify required artifacts separately, each with identity, description, source and owning producer. A planned artifact is not evidence it exists. A useful prerequisite may be its own task when it owes an independently deliverable result needed earlier; do not make that result wait for its own consumer's completion.
5. Each candidate retains its own ideation, planning, implementation and verification responsibilities when delivered. Those phase names do not become four separate candidates. This method does not settle detailed implementation choices.
6. Bind existing question identities to the candidates that must settle or carry them. Preserve source, answer owner, blocked role or none, and known answers. A planner-owned design question may travel to planning; a missing execution authorization is not resolved by naming a candidate.

Ground claims in actual available source and distinguish interpretation from facts. Keep source identity distinct from locator, requirement from intent, condition from proof, and candidate from artifact. Carry applicable Rumen fences, independent judgment, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners; an acknowledgment/hash is not a passed check. No candidate grants authority, access, publication, spending, selection or dispatch.

Use NQ in agent exchanges: state understanding, reuse scoped answers, ask concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs only, and retain answered-turn count. After three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. Preserve private tracks. Retain contributions before handoff in the existing record when supported; disclose absent persistence and recover saved results before retrying interrupted work.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** produce coherent outcome candidates plus necessary independently owed preparation, complete coverage, conditions and question bindings from available material.
- **Focused:** additionally examine a material grouping/splitting alternative against ownership, overlap and early prerequisite usefulness; record why the chosen boundaries hold.
- **Deep:** additionally walk difficult outcome and failure cases through the decomposition using accessible primary material, exposing missing ownership or dependency cycles and revising candidate boundaries explicitly.

Report requested, authorized/effective and performed effort with actual comparisons and limits. Public standalone use is unrestricted by managed commerce. Managed paid Focused/Deep authorization belongs to the service; missing access/capability requires disclosure and an explicit alternative, never silent substitution. Every tier retains correctness, safety, privacy, required evidence and recovery; effort does not choose a provider or expand authority.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown candidates with stable identities, source/requirement coverage, artifacts/conditions, unresolved question owners and proposed prerequisite needs. Prepare the next selected consumer with those records and resolvable permitted sources; no sibling package or invented successor is required. Stop when all sought results are faithfully accounted for or a concrete gap prevents it. Dependency routing and final confirmation may refine the account explicitly; neither can silently erase it. Selection and execution remain separate authorized acts.

**Example:** “Provide a searchable archive” may need a records-retention decision first. Keep candidate C-archive as the sought capability and, if independently owed, C-policy as its prerequisite artifact. Do not return only “investigate retention” and call the archive request covered.
