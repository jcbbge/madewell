# Work

Start where the work actually is. Preserve its intended outcome, deliberate choices, exact material, and unfinished obligations rather than rebuilding an intake on each turn.

This is an unpublished portable method proposal, not an accepted shipped default or a running workflow service.

## Choose the useful component

The opinionated outer default is **Brainstorm → Discovery → Delivery → Landing**. Delivery's default is **Ideation → Planning → Implementation → Verification**, for each deliberately selected task. These are component definitions, not mandatory agent calls, task counts, or approval stops. A component may be used alone, repeated, extended, or replaced by a compatible composition that preserves its enclosing purpose and input/output obligations. Distinguish a method definition, its occurrence in a composition, an actual attempt, and the task receiving its contribution.

- Select Brainstorm to develop a lead or framing. Its supplied entry method pairs with ambitious collaboration; select specialists for the obstruction, not the whole toolbox.
- Select Discovery to shape rough material into sourced candidates. Its default methods are Declare, lookFirst, classify, Lenses, oneThing, route, confirm. The separate Discovery guide selects those methods.
- Select Delivery for one selected task's fulfillment, or select an individual responsibility when that is the actual request. The separate Delivery guide selects its four methods.
- Select Landing to return a completed unit with its actual delta, learning, propagation and correction cost. The separate Landing method preserves the recovered historical four-face contract under current durable, domain-independent boundaries; it does not authorize release, deletion or task promotion.

Method names here identify possible selections, not file links or instructions to read sibling packages. Use only explicitly available instructions through the host's supported loading path. An unavailable explicit selection is a visible gap, not permission to substitute another method. This guide does not perform or dispatch the selected methods by naming them.

## Keep the work intact

Recover the actual source and relevant prior contributions, decisions, questions and answers before asking the person to reconstruct them. Rough notes are valid input. Preserve original text behind summaries; distinguish source statements from interpretation, assumptions, and observations. Keep existing identities for intent, requirements, dispositions, candidates, artifacts, questions, dependencies, conditions and evidence. Give new records distinguishable identities without silently renumbering earlier ones.

Ground connects claims to available material. Rumen accounts for applicable mechanical fences, independent judgment, operator-owned guidance and project vocabulary with their evidence owners. They span work, not extra serial phases. Never turn a fingerprint, rule acknowledgment, valid output shape or maker's claim into evidence that a check passed.

Selecting a candidate is an explicit commitment, not dispatch or execution permission. Show dependency consequences before selection; do not infer all candidates or prerequisites were selected. Skill loading, effort, tools, paid access and authority over work/data are separate. Do not grant actions or private context by composing methods.

Every agent-to-agent exchange uses NQ: state understanding, reuse scoped answers, ask only concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs, and let the sender answer without silently changing the brief. No ritual questions are needed for a clear assignment. Preserve the exchange and answered-turn count; after three answered turns with unresolved questions, hold that work for a specification defect and alert the responsible human through the actual channel. Do not reset the count by changing agents.

Use the existing work record when available; retain transitions before a successor proceeds. Without a persistence facility, return the continuity material and disclose that it is not durably saved. On interruption, recover recorded inputs, results and receipts before acting; an uncertain external effect is not permission to repeat it. Keep private tracks private and preserve rejected/partial attempts.

## Effort and return

Light is the free default: recover the current objective, valid evidence and next useful component, with its owner and any missing input. Focused additionally reconciles conflicting/stale contributions at the next handoff and records what changes. Deep additionally traces a completion-critical dependency through available original material and historical attempts to prepare a precise continuation boundary. Do not repeat accepted work to perform a deeper orientation.

Record requested, authorized/effective and actually performed effort separately, citing the material and reconciliation done. Public standalone use is unrestricted by managed commerce. Managed Focused/Deep access is authorized by the service, not these instructions; unavailable access/capabilities require disclosure and an explicit alternative, not silent substitution. No effort level weakens required correctness, safety, privacy, evidence or recovery, changes provider settings, or expands authority.

Return natural Markdown: intended outcome; evidenced result versus remaining gap; actual activity (or stopped/unknown); next action and owner; whether human input is needed. Prepare only selected successors with usable permitted source and your actual contribution; terminal work need not invent one. Stop orientation when the next justified action is clear or a precise missing input blocks it. Do not call unowned or unobserved work “in flight.”

**Example:** A resumed campaign task has a reviewed draft but no authorized send. Resume from the draft and unresolved send authority; do not restart discovery, claim the campaign launched, or send it merely because Delivery is selected.
