# confirm

Confirm that the candidate set faithfully accounts for the request. Confirmation is not a fabricated user “yes,” task selection, execution permission or proof that any candidate is delivered.

## Apply

Receive exact original source and the available intent, observation, classification, tradeoff, candidate and routing contributions, with settled answers and uncertainty. Used alone, reconcile the supplied candidate set against its actual source and disclose missing earlier contributions rather than claiming a complete sequence. Do not require sibling packages to be read.

1. Account for every intent through linked requirements or explicit sourced dispositions (provided, context, deferred, superseded). Keep original intent identities as traceable anchors. Explain clarifications and newly emerging outcomes by source and rationale; do not silently rename an anchor or turn interpretation into instruction.
2. Ensure every requirement retains candidate ownership. Preserve coherent desired-result candidates alongside prerequisites. A comparison, clarification or evidence inventory alone does not discharge a sought capability. Unapproved or blocked outcomes stay visible; any declined/unresolved coverage needs an explicit source-grounded reason, not convenience.
3. Retain candidate identities and kinds, requirement ownership, artifact identities/producers/source links, condition identities and all accepted question bindings. Clarifying prose is not permission to change those commitments. Give new conditions or questions distinct identities and provenance; preserve question owners, blocked roles, answers and prior bindings. All questions remain addressable, including those not blocking work.
4. Preserve every accepted dependency identity/edge/reason, artifact linkage and preparation promise. The final dependency graph must be the accepted routing graph, not a fresh graph reconstructed from memory. Display ordering may change only without changing those edges. Do not promise within-task readiness unsupported by the actual contract.
5. If reconciliation exposes a changed outcome, missing candidate, conflicting ownership or bad edge, return the precise affected contribution for explicit revision. Preserve prior attempts and do not silently filter a contradiction to make the list look finished. Do not invent a predetermined task count or wording to reproduce.

Each candidate needs usable purpose, scope/exclusions, source/requirement links, identifiable observable completion conditions, required artifacts, dependencies/readiness inputs and unresolved questions. Keep source identity distinct from locator and actual observations distinct from assertions. Ground the reconciliation in inspectable material; carry applicable Rumen fences, independent judgments, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners. Tidy references, hashes or valid shape do not prove semantic fidelity or executed checks.

Use NQ for agent exchanges: state understanding, reuse scoped answers, ask concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs only, and preserve answered turns across replacements. After three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. Keep private material within the recipient's grant. Retain the reconciliation before handoff in the existing record when supported; disclose absent durability and recover saved contributions before retrying interrupted work.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** reconcile all supplied intent/requirement coverage, candidate/artifact/question identities and the complete accepted graph; expose every remaining gap.
- **Focused:** additionally compare an ambiguous outcome or condition with concrete source examples and reconcile its consequences without identity loss.
- **Deep:** additionally trace difficult end-to-end coverage and dependency paths back to accessible primary material and adverse cases, explicitly revising or returning unsupported commitments.

Report requested, authorized/effective and performed effort with actual reconciliation and limits. Standalone public use is unrestricted by managed commerce. Managed paid Focused/Deep authorization comes from the service; disclose unavailable access/capability and offer an explicit alternative, not silent substitution. No tier weakens correctness, safety, privacy, required evidence or recovery, selects a provider, or expands authority.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown: final candidates and conditions, explicit coverage/dispositions, preserved dependency/preparation/question references, unresolved gaps/owners, actual effort, and usable permitted material for the next selected consumer. Stop at faithful candidate reconciliation or a precise unresolved defect. A terminal discovery result need not invent a successor.

If a person later selects work, show exact selected IDs and prerequisite consequences. Do not infer an empty selection means all, automatically include prerequisites, launch agents, or implement. A complete candidate list is not a commitment to deliver it.

**Example:** C-reminders depends on C-consent and carries Q-channel. Confirmation retains both and Q-channel even if only C-consent looks ready. It does not quietly drop C-reminders, answer Q-channel, or promote either task.
