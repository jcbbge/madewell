# Implementation

Execute the accepted fixed brief. If it cannot be followed, return the concrete defect through its question path rather than improvising a new plan or expanding authority.

## Apply

Receive the actual fixed brief/version, selected task and artifact identities, permitted source and resources, settled decisions/answers, scope and exclusions, current conditions, applicable rules, dependencies and recovery obligations. This method works alone with a real supplied brief; do not fabricate prior approvals or require sibling skills. Missing execution authority or a missing consequential choice prevents the affected action, not honest retention of partial work.

1. Confirm the receiving material is the version the brief actually uses and within the recipient's grant. Ground against required actual inputs after setup/restoration. Keep source identity distinct from locator/version; unavailable inputs are not absent requirements.
2. Carry out only the agreed actions on authorized targets. Preserve intent, requirement/disposition, artifact, condition, dependency and question identities. Follow existing patterns and boundaries specified by the brief; do not quietly choose a different architecture, outcome or prerequisite.
3. Account for applicable Rumen rules: carry mechanical fences for their authorized executor, independent judgment for its assigned reviewer, operator-owned guidance and project vocabulary. Show how the artifact addresses them without awarding a gate pass. Guidance, hashes and maker claims are not evidence of executed checks.
4. Preserve actual changed/created artifacts and action receipts, not merely descriptions of intended work. Identify exact locations/versions, attempted versus completed actions, partials, failures and known limitations. A planned file, sent command or timed-out request does not prove the result exists or failed.
5. Do not author or run this work's independent acceptance checks, inspect the private check-design track, or certify your own result as independently verified. For software, plan-required in-scope maintenance of existing regression tests is distinct from independent acceptance-check authorship; it grants no check-execution permission. Follow the actual brief's ownership boundaries, not a renamed profile.
6. If a plan defect or unexpected fact blocks execution, retain the exact partial result and evidence. Name the blocked action, question identity/source, responsible answer owner and required revision/input. Keep the intended result; do not deliver an easier research task instead.

Every agent exchange uses NQ: state understanding, reuse scoped answers, ask only concrete unclear/ambiguous/missing inputs, preserve answers and answered-turn count across replacements. After three answered turns with unresolved questions hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human; no fourth agent-only round or reset. Missing authority and safety boundaries stop the action immediately rather than waiting for three turns.

Retain transitions, artifacts/partials and exchanges in the existing work record before a successor proceeds when available; disclose absent persistence. On interruption recover saved results and effect receipts before acting. An uncertain external effect requires inspection/reconciliation under the same logical identity, not blind repetition with a fresh ID. Changed plans require explicit carry-forward and new preparation; do not overwrite history or reuse stale proof. Clean up only owned disposable resources after preserving required material.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** perform the complete accepted brief faithfully, retaining actual artifacts, failures and required evidence/preparation. Light is not permission for partial correctness.
- **Focused:** when already included in the fixed brief, additionally retain its specified intermediate checkpoints and critical-path observations. If none were included, report that no additional Focused work is established for this brief.
- **Deep:** only when already included in the fixed brief, retain its specified evidence through a difficult transformation or failure/recovery sequence. Otherwise no distinct Deep implementation recipe exists; do not manufacture work or reopen design to justify a label.

Keep requested, authorized/effective and actually performed effort separate; cite real checkpoints/artifacts and limits. Public standalone use is unrestricted by managed commerce; managed paid work needs external service authorization. Missing access/capability or an unsupported requested variant needs disclosure and an explicit alternative, not silent substitution. No effort weakens correctness, safety, privacy, required evidence or recovery, selects a provider, or grants extra scope, publication, contact, spending or deployment.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown: actual result/artifacts and precise changes/actions; brief/version followed; partials/failures/questions with owners; condition evidence actually available and checks not run; actual effort; usable permitted material for each selected verifier/consumer. Preserve private-track isolation; do not require that a checker read your inaccessible private report to establish correctness. A standalone return need not invent a successor.

Stop at completed authorized making or a precise blocked action. Source-ready, reported complete, independently accepted and live usable are different claims; automatic release is not part of this method.

**Example:** A brief authorizes drafting a pricing sheet, not updating the store. Return the actual sheet and any unresolved figures; do not publish it or mark customer-facing prices verified because the draft exists.

Unpublished method proposal; source output is not behavioral or native-host acceptance.
