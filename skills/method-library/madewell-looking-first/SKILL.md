# lookFirst

Read the wall before adding a brick: establish what exists, what it supports and what a change must preserve. Existing structure is evidence, not an automatic veto on change.

## Apply

Receive the intended outcome or rough question, exact source, authorized artifacts, prior decisions and answers, and actual inspection capabilities. No repository, software stack or industry rule is implied. This method works alone; absent a prior intent contribution, state the supplied question without fabricating predecessor work.

1. Inspect the relevant current material, instructions, interfaces and capabilities in proportion to the requested change and its consequence. Prefer primary artifacts over summaries where the distinction matters.
2. Give findings stable observation identities. Retain each source identity separately from its exact locator/version, the observation it supports, and inspection limits. Distinguish “the document states X,” “I observed X,” and “I infer X.” Preserve existing source and observation identities; new versions do not overwrite earlier evidence.
3. Explain how findings constrain the intended work: reusable capability, preserved behavior, obsolete choice, relevant absence actually established, or unresolved evidence. Merely listing files is not understanding.
4. Reconcile existing work and known answers before proposing duplicates. Keep contradictory observations and changed circumstances visible. Missing access means **unavailable evidence**, not “the feature does not exist.” Name the smallest further observation that would resolve a consequential gap.

Keep question identities, sources, answer owners and any blocking roles. Ground claims in actual inspection; carry applicable Rumen mechanical fences, independent judgment, operator guidance and vocabulary with their evidence owners. A source hash establishes bytes, not truth or a passed check. Inspection is not permission to edit, publish, spend or access private sibling material.

Every agent exchange uses NQ: confirm purpose/scope, reuse scoped answers, ask only concrete missing/ambiguous inputs, preserve count and answers. After three answered turns with questions unresolved, hold the affected work for a specification defect and alert its responsible human. Do not restart intake on an agent replacement. Preserve the contribution in the existing record before a successor proceeds when supported; disclose absent durability. Recover saved findings and uncertain-effect receipts before repeating an interrupted action.

## Effort

- **Light — free default:** inspect the directly relevant available material and state existing capabilities, constraints and evidence gaps.
- **Focused:** additionally follow an affected consumer/interface or conflicting observation to establish its consequence for the proposed change.
- **Deep:** additionally trace a decisive behavior into accessible primary implementation/history or real examples, compare an adverse case, and revise the account of what must be preserved.

Report requested, authorized/effective and performed effort with actual material and findings. Public standalone use is unrestricted by managed commerce; paid managed Focused/Deep authorization is external to this skill. Disclose unavailable access/capability and offer an explicit alternative without silently changing the request. Every level retains required correctness, safety, privacy, evidence and recovery; effort neither sets a provider nor expands authority.

## Return and boundary

Return natural Markdown: observations with source links/limits; reusable existing work; constraints and invariants; genuine unknowns/questions with owners; consequence for the next selected method; actual effort. Prepare that consumer with the inspectable material it may access and your actual contribution, not only a generic summary. Stop once findings support the next useful transformation or a precise gap blocks it.

Do not redesign or implement here. A plausible replacement or completed investigation is not a delivered outcome.

**Example:** A supplied screenshot lacks an export button, but the settings page is inaccessible. Report the observed screenshot and the uninspected settings boundary; do not conclude exports are absent and create a duplicate export project.

Unpublished method proposal; source output is not behavioral or native-host acceptance.
