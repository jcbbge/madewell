# Landing

Return one completed unit of work cleanly to its surrounding work: retain what changed, what doing it taught, what must be reconciled, and the actual cost of corrections. Land per unit, not merely at session end. Landing is separate from Delivery's Verification; a green check or merged change alone does not finish this return.

## Ground the return

Receive the selected unit's identity, exact intent and settled decisions, actual artifacts and verification evidence, unfinished propagation, relevant answers and permitted destinations. Recover an existing partial record before asking for material already available. Distinguish accepted evidence, external reports and unavailable observations. If the unit's completion is unestablished, return that gap and the partial closure material; do not manufacture a completed unit.

Use only authorized material. A selected method, paid access or a recorded result is not permission to publish, deploy, send, delete, promote candidates, release another owner's claims or write to external systems. Retain earlier inputs, briefs, results and receipts in the existing work record; historical file-deletion instructions do not apply to durable evidence.

## Return the four faces

- **DELTA:** identify the actual unit and result, with accessible artifact/evidence references and what changed. A diff or release can be evidence when applicable; non-software work does not require Git. Say what actually reached its destination and what remains unobserved. Do not call a draft published or deployment complete because its artifact passed verification.
- **LEARNED:** name the useful thing doing this revealed that was not already recorded. Route work knowledge to its authorized decision/memory/vocabulary surface, or process knowledge to its responsible process-improvement owner. If nothing real surfaced, write `none`. A proposed destination is not a completed write.
- **PROPAGATED:** reconcile the existing surfaces affected by this delta, such as documentation, current work/queue, source dispositions and vocabulary. For each relevant surface state `done` with evidence, `n/a` with reason, or `owed` with the actual owner and a durable work reference where one exists. Missing ownership or tracking remains an explicit gap. Deferred propagation is visible debt, not an automatic refusal of the Land record and not permission to promote or execute follow-up work. New findings remain possible work until deliberately selected.
- **TAX:** report correction cost only from actual evidence. With comparable first-pass and accepted measurements, retain both, the consistent unit and sources, and their difference. With only an actual correction diff, identify that direct measure and its limits; do not invent first-pass counts. If measurement is unavailable, say `unknown` and why, not zero or a retrospective estimate disguised as a measurement. Keep units consistent within the relevant work stream. Warning counts, confidence, elapsed time and response length are not correction cost and must not be added to Tax.

Only legitimately deferrable propagation becomes owed debt. An unmet condition required to close the actual unit, missing authorization or an uncertain external effect remains a completion gap; labeling it `owed` does not discharge it. Comparable artifact sizes alone need not measure corrections: name the metric, first-pass/accepted artifact identities or direct correction evidence, and the measurement basis.

Keep warnings separate from the measured Tax. A missing field, unpropagated surface or unavailable measurement is a visible diagnostic, not a new blocking gate invented by this method. Existing acceptance, authority and safety gates still apply. Do not run a historical checker or write a tax ledger merely because this skill mentions one.

## Handoff and stop

Return natural Markdown with the four faces, attributable evidence and any owed action/owner. Retain exact source material behind summaries when the host supports it; without persistence return the record and disclose that it is not durably saved. Distinguish the closure record from actions it recommends and from independently verified business completion.

For an agent exchange, state understanding, reuse scoped answers and preserve the NQ answered-turn count. Ask only a concrete missing input that inspection cannot recover. After three answered turns with genuine unresolved questions, hold the affected exchange for a specification defect and alert its responsible human; do not restart intake to evade the hold. Never read private sibling material to fill a gap.

Light is the free default and performs the complete return above at the rigor its evidence requires. This extracted method supplies no separate Focused/Deep recipe; do not invent one, downgrade required evidence or claim extra work from a paid label. Provider reasoning, output length and external-action authority remain separate.

Stop when the unit's return and remaining debt are explicit and retained through the actual available channel, or when a precise completion-critical gap prevents faithful closure. Do not invent a successor, new task board, deletion or deployment to make the record look finished.

For source attribution and the translation from historical software-specific mechanics, read [historical contract](references/historical-contract.md).

**Example:** The completed unit is producing a verified printed notice; posting is outside that unit's required conditions and an authorized person has not posted it. DELTA identifies the accepted notice, not a completed posting. LEARNED can be `none`; PROPAGATED identifies the posting owner and retained owed reference; TAX is `unknown` if no first-pass or correction evidence survives. Recording this return neither posts the notice nor marks that external outcome verified. If posting were a required condition of this unit, it would instead remain a completion gap.
