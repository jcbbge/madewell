# Explore: The Dawn-Side Instrument — Grounding as Rumen's Mirror

**Status:** note, v0 — flagged 2026-07-18 at session close; to be tackled a
different day. Formal definition owed.

## The observation

The runtime constitution has an asymmetry. The Verify/Land side of the loop
has a dedicated instrument: **Rumen** — hosted through a one-way contract,
arming Orion's criteria and Aldebaran's judgment with fences, judges, and
the lexicon, fed back by the acceptance-differential tax sensor at Land.

The Ideate/Plan side (Dawn/Meridian — Vega, Polaris, Cassiopeia) has no
named counterpart. The work entering the houses deserves the same class of
instrumentation as the work leaving them.

## The candidate is already on the shelf

We likely already have this organ in pieces — it needs a formal definition,
not an invention:

- **Grounding for Speech** (`./EXPLORE-epistemics.md`) — claims as typed
  values, acquisition-before-assertion as a scheduler constraint.
- **Plan-grounding** (`../contact-points.md`) — "the plan is woven *from*
  striation threads; there is no plan without them."
- Both are the same design move as Rumen's fences: material supplied by the
  pipe, not checks offered to the agent.

The symmetry, stated: **Rumen governs what leaves the loop (quality of
output); the grounding instrument governs what enters it (quality of
input — facts, striations, prior art, intent).** Rumen prevents the
near-miss; the dawn instrument prevents the ungrounded start. Same
one-way-contract shape, opposite end of the pipe.

## Also settled this session (nomenclature)

Domain expertise ships as a **cartridge**; the kernel/runtime is the
**console** (consistent with `../rubric.md`). "Pack" is released — it no
longer names the persona/domain artifact, resolving the collision with
Rumen's excreted packs.

## Open questions for the formal definition

- Is the dawn instrument one engine (grounding) or two (grounding for
  claims + striation supply for plans)?
- Does it get a metabolism like Rumen's (sources earn/lose residence in the
  fact sheet), or is freshness its only law?
- What is its tax sensor — the Dawn-side equivalent of the acceptance
  differential? (Candidate: plan assumptions that Verify later falsifies.)
- Who hosts whom: does the console call it at Dawn/Meridian the way the
  host calls Rumen at Verify/Land?
