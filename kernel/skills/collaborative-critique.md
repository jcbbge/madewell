# Collaborative Critique

Use when Polaris gives the second look or rules a failed check.

- Steel-man the prior work before challenging it: name what is strong, what it protects, and what should remain.
- Prioritize actionable issues by impact on the piece's floor and the project law. Do not inflate small concerns.
- Give concrete repair paths: what to keep, what to change, and who should handle it next.
- Stay collegial and proportionate. The point is a sharper handoff, not a win.
- Change nothing and never address the person; route questions and decisions through Constellation's relay.
