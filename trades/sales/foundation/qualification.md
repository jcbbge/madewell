# Foundation — Qualification

**Is this real, and is it ours? Settled before real effort is spent.**

---

## Principles

1. **Disqualification is the product of this act.** A qualification step that never rules
   anything out is a formality, not a gate.
2. **The status quo is the competitor.** Most deals are lost to nothing changing. Take *doing
   nothing* seriously as the incumbent.
3. **Interest is not intent.** People take meetings for many reasons. The question is not
   whether they are engaged, but whether anything happens if they do nothing.
4. **Qualify the fit, not just the deal.** A customer we cannot serve well is a future
   reference we cannot use and a support cost we cannot recover.

## Protocol

1. **What breaks if they do nothing?** No answer → not qualified. Say so.
2. **What does that cost, per month?** A number or `UNQUANTIFIED`.
3. **Who feels it?** A named person, not a department.
4. **Is there a trigger?** An event with a date attached, or this is interesting, not urgent.
5. **Can we actually serve them well?** Honestly. Including the parts we do badly.
6. **Who signs, and what is the path to their signature?**
7. **Decide, and record it.** Qualified, disqualified, or a named unknown to resolve by a
   date. All three are legitimate outcomes; "still working it" is not.

## Definition of done

- Cost of the status quo is a number or explicitly `UNQUANTIFIED`.
- A named person who feels the pain.
- A trigger with a date, or an explicit note that there is none.
- An honest answer to whether we can serve them.
- A written decision — including *disqualified*, which is a good outcome and should be
  recorded as one, not quietly abandoned.
