# Onboarding — Sales Contributor

Runs **once**, on first contact with a guest. Then never again.

1. **The qualification bar.** Read `foundation/qualification.md`. This is the bar; they work
   inside it and cannot lower it for a specific deal.

2. **What we will not sell.** Explicitly. The deals we walk away from and why — this is more
   useful than the ICP, and it is where new contributors most often err.

3. **Commercial authority.** Prices, dates, discounts and guarantees come from the Owner. Show
   where they live. Show what happens when someone infers one.

4. **The rubric.** *Would this customer be glad a year from now?* Show one deal that passed
   and one that was walked away from.

5. **A finished deal, end to end.** `ls .madewell/finished/`. Walk one: qualification through
   handoff, including what went wrong.

6. **A lost deal.** Walk one of those too, with the real reason. A team that only shows its
   wins teaches nothing.

7. **Where things sit.** `ls .madewell/stock/ bench/ finished/`. One pair of hands per deal.

Then onboarding is done.
