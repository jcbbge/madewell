# Discovery Lenses — Sales

**Extends:** the kernel's `discovery` skill (`.madewell/skills/discovery.md`), via its Lens Slot.
**When:** loaded with the sales trade; run *after* the universal core on every pass.

The engine lives in the kernel. This adds what a sales lane can see that the universal core
cannot name.

---

## Lens S1 — Cost of the status quo

- What it costs today: frequency × duration × people × rate. Push until it is a number.
- What they have already spent working around it — headcount, tools, overtime.
- Whether the cost is rising, and what makes it rise.
- Absence of a cost is the finding: this is a preference, not a problem.

## Lens S2 — Decision path

- Who signs. Who influences. Who can veto silently.
- What has to be true before a signature: legal, security, procurement, a board.
- How they bought the last thing like this. The pattern repeats.
- Who was not in the room, and why.

## Lens S3 — Status quo inertia

- What they do today, in steps.
- Why it persists — who built it, who defends it, whose job it is.
- What they already tried, and how it failed. This is the constraint map.
- Who would look bad if this changed. That person is a risk with a name.

## Lens S4 — Commercial signal

- Budget: does it exist, whose is it, what cycle.
- Timing: a date attached to something real, or a hope.
- Language shifts from "if" to "when", and when exactly that happened.
- What they compare the price to. That comparison is the real competitor.

## Lens S5 — Post-signature risk

- What would make this fail *after* they buy.
- Who has to change how they work, and were they in the room.
- What was promised informally that is not in the contract.
- What delivery will discover that sales did not ask.
