# Pillar — Handoff

*What delivery inherits.*

## Principles

1. **The customer must never repeat themselves.** Everything they told you in the deal is
   theirs, not yours; losing it is a breach of the relationship, not an admin failure.
2. **Hand over the promises, especially the informal ones.** What was said on a call binds the
   company. If it is not written down, it will be broken.
3. **Hand over the doubts.** What worried you about this deal is the most useful thing
   delivery can receive, and the least likely to be sent.
4. **A handoff is a conversation, not a document.** The document is the artifact; the
   conversation is the handoff.

## Protocol

1. Write what they are trying to achieve, in their words, from discovery.
2. List every commitment made — contractual and verbal. Especially verbal.
3. Name the people: who signed, who uses it, who was sceptical.
4. Name what worried you and what you would watch.
5. Hold the conversation. Delivery asks questions; answer them then.
6. Introduce delivery to the customer **before** you step back.
7. Record it, and finish the piece.

## Definition of done

- Their goal is recorded in their own words.
- Verbal commitments are written down.
- The sceptic is named.
- Delivery has asked questions and had them answered.
- The customer has met the delivery team while sales is still present.
