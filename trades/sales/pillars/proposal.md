# Pillar — Proposal

*The shape of the offer, priced and bounded.*

## Principles

1. **A proposal is an argument, not a price list.** It restates their problem in their words,
   then shows what changes. Price is the last page, not the document.
2. **Bound it explicitly.** What is out of scope is as load-bearing as what is in. Ambiguity
   here becomes a delivery dispute later.
3. **No invented terms.** Every price, date and guarantee traces to the Owner.
4. **Their words, not ours.** If they do not recognise their own problem on page one, nothing
   after it matters.

## Protocol

1. Restate the problem, quoted from discovery. Confirm it before proposing anything.
2. State what changes, in their terms — outcomes, not features.
3. Bound it: in scope, out of scope, assumptions.
4. Price it from the Owner's terms. Never derive a price by analogy to another deal.
5. Name the risks honestly, including ours.
6. Say what happens next, with dates.
7. Run `skills/deal-review.md` **before** it is sent.

## Definition of done

- Page one is their problem in their words.
- Out-of-scope is written and specific.
- Every commercial term traces to the Owner.
- Deal review has run and its stop conditions are clear.
