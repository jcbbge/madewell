# Onboarding — Marketing Contributor

Runs **once**, on first contact with a guest contributor. Then never again.

The point is that the system reads out its own accumulated reality, so the Owner never has to
become the onboarding teacher.

1. **The positioning.** Read `foundation/positioning.md` aloud, in full. It is one paragraph,
   and everything they make will inherit it. If it does not exist, stop — that is the first
   piece of work, and it is the Owner's.

2. **The audience's own words.** Show the captured verbatim phrases (`pillars/audience.md`).
   Contributors reach for company vocabulary by default; this is the antidote.

3. **What is already claimed, and sourced.** Walk the existing claims and their sources. New
   work either reuses a sourced claim or brings its own source.

4. **The voice.** Show two assets that are on-voice and one that is not, and say why.

5. **Where things sit.** `ls .madewell/stock/ bench/ finished/`. The rack is large on purpose;
   the bench is short on purpose.

6. **The line they do not cross.** They work inside the positioning. If they think it is
   wrong — and they may be right — that is a finding for the Owner, raised as a piece on the
   rack, not a change made in an asset.

Then onboarding is done.
