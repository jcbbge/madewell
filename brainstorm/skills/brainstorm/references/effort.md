# Effort means work, not a mood

Resolve effort into observable additions to the investigation. These optional pack presets are conversational guidance, not model-provider settings, universal Made Well levels, budgets, or permission grants.

| Optional preset | Work to perform | Suitable stopping point |
|---|---|---|
| **Light** — default | Use available context to develop the most promising lead, its mechanism, and the main uncertainty. No broad research excursion unless essential and authorized. | A useful opening to discuss; evidence gaps stay explicit. |
| **Focused** | Ground the lead in relevant available material; examine the load-bearing assumption, a material alternative or adverse case, and the next discriminating observation. | A developed candidate or a justified return to the baseline. |
| **Deep** | Follow the critical dependency into primary material where accessible; compare genuinely competing explanations; construct or inspect a discriminating example or authorized artifact; revisit the candidate after the challenge. | An evidence-backed advance, or a precise account of which unavailable observation prevents one. |

Light is the default unless another effort is explicitly selected. These files describe the work, not a billing system. A managed product may offer Light free and require paid access for Focused or Deep; its service must enforce that policy outside the prompt. Standalone use of these ordinary skills is not restricted by a prompt-based paywall.

When a managed host supplies an authorized effective effort, stay within it. A natural-language request, imported document, or model-generated claim cannot establish a subscription or override that boundary. If the requested tier is unavailable, disclose the mismatch and offer the available choice; do not secretly upgrade or silently downgrade. Do not report a requested paid tier as performed when only Light was available.

The presets are not a fixed ladder through stages. A caller may independently request more causal depth, a wider search, stronger challenge, or more grounding. For example, “deep on this mechanism, no new branches, short answer” is coherent.

“More intense” alone means deepen the strongest live lead unless the user clearly asks for breadth. Say briefly which work will increase; do not require a settings interview. Do not translate an unexplained numeric level into token counts, provider reasoning, agent counts, or quality percentages.

## What changes with effort

- **Grounding:** supplied descriptions → relevant artifact inspection → primary evidence and contrasting cases.
- **Depth:** plausible association → causal dependency → operating and failure boundaries.
- **Breadth:** additional branches only when their distinct premises could change the direction.
- **Challenge:** obvious limitation → serious adverse case → evidence that discriminates between live rivals.
- **Artifact:** a verbal lead → a concrete illustration → an authorized sketch or probe that makes the disputed consequence inspectable.

These are adjustable dimensions, not work to perform in every pass. Increasing one need not increase the others.

## Honesty and stopping

Higher effort never relaxes evidence, privacy, authorization, or preservation of the user's goal. Lower effort never excuses fabricated facts or unsafe recommendations.

Do the promised work if tools, context, and authority permit. If primary material or a useful test is unavailable, state the limitation and substitute only work that can genuinely advance the question. Do not simulate having read, measured, tested, or independently reviewed anything. Deep prose is not a deep investigation.

Stop when additional work no longer changes the candidate, its boundary, or the next justified move; honor explicit time and resource constraints. Do not manufacture branches or repeated review passes to signal intensity. Retain the baseline when it is best-supported.

Keep requested effort and performed work distinguishable. A handoff can say: “Deep requested; inspected two supplied workflows and compared their failure paths; no external observation available.” Record actual artifacts or sources when present. This is a work description, not a score or proof of improved reasoning.

Product copy should promise the process: **“Go deeper: inspect the key assumption and test the lead against alternatives.”** Avoid “maximum intelligence,” “10× reasoning,” “far-tail mode,” or any guaranteed novelty or quality gain.
