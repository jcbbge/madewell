# Brainstorm method map

Choose by what is obstructing the conversation, not by a fixed order. These are independently callable skills, not mandatory steps or a fleet of agents. Load only the selected skill's actual instructions through the host's supported mechanism.

The names below refer to the sibling skills in the library. A standalone installation may omit any of them; do not fabricate their instructions or claim an unavailable skill was activated.

## The central distinctions

| Skill | Distinct job | Reach for it when |
|---|---|---|
| `ambitious-collaboration` | Maintain an expansive but grounded posture | The conversation needs permission to notice more than the first plausible answer. |
| `find-the-unlock` | Deepen a lead into a specific enabling change | Something promising is visible, but the leverage or next investigation is vague. |
| `nonconvergence` | Change the generating premise | Alternatives keep reproducing the same causal approach. |
| `right-side-of-the-curve` | Find the smallest sufficient mechanism, including a better representation | Capability is getting buried under machinery or an enabling simplification is possible. |

Nonconvergence is not a higher effort setting. Right-side-of-the-curve is not a low-ambition setting. An unconventional approach can be simpler; a familiar approach can be both sufficient and best-supported. Use these moves independently or in either order.

## Further moves already in the library

| Obstruction or invitation | Skill | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| The stated problem may embed the wrong solution | `reframing-problems` | Separate desired outcome from assumed route. |
| We need genuinely different possibilities | `exploring-possibilities` | Open branches distinguished by their mechanisms. |
| A deliberate large idea sweep is requested | `idea-wizard` | Generate and compare a batch; its default 30-to-5 format is not every brainstorm's quota. |
| We need to make the problem inspectable | `cognitive-approaches` | Externalize structure, constraints, or dynamics. |
| Another field may have the missing operation | `creative-systems-thinking` | Transfer a mechanism with applicability and failure boundaries. |
| The same problem keeps reproducing itself | `thinking-systemically` | Find the relevant feedback, delay, stock, or displaced cost. |
| A valuable contribution is hard to see | `ablation` | Trace what breaks and what gets blamed if a function is absent. |
| A consequential premise is too comfortable | `challenging-assumptions` | Find the hinge and evidence that could overturn it. |
| A perspective or failure path is missing | `detecting-blind-spots` | Surface material omissions without inventing absent safeguards. |
| A discovery might enable something else | `mapping-adjacent-possibilities` | Distinguish enabled, conditional, and speculative next possibilities. |
| Sources need connecting without forced agreement | `synthesizing-insights` | Find a relationship that survives differences and provenance checks. |
| A supported choice is needed | `think-critically` | Resolve delegated tradeoffs rather than endlessly expanding the option set. |
| The exploration has become inert or scattered | `criticality` | Adjust observable working conditions, not imagined neural parameters. |
| An intuitive, oracle-like reading is requested | `luck` | Offer explicitly interpretive leads, not factual claims about hidden motives. |
| Existing scaffolding may have outlived its purpose | `buoyant` | Identify removal conditions; an audit is not authorization to delete. |

## Composition by need

A thread might start with ambient ambition, deepen one lead with find-the-unlock, and use right-side-of-the-curve to expose its minimal form. Another might begin with ablation and never need divergent ideation. These are illustrations, not recipes.

Do not run critique after every half-sentence, and do not postpone all challenge until a persuasive story hardens. Develop enough of the candidate to reveal what would change, then test the relevant boundary. Preserve a useful partial insight even when it is not ready to recommend.

Specialist defaults remain their own. Resolve an explicitly requested count or effort override visibly; do not silently replace a user's chosen method with the pack's preferred style.
