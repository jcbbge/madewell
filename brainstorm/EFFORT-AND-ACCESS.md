# Effort and commercial access

## Settled direction versus proposed implementation

**Operator direction:** ship Light processes as the free defaults; put Focused and Deep behind paid access. This Brainstorm seed supplies concrete profiles for that direction. The operator named [Rams pricing](https://www.rams.ai/pricing) as a reference, not as authority to copy prices or buy a service.

**Recommendation:** start with free access to Light and one paid entitlement that unlocks both Focused and Deep. Keep the effort choice separate from commercial plan names so packaging and pricing can evolve without rewriting skills.

**Not chosen:** prices, currency, billing period, payment provider, checkout flow, account/team identity, included usage, overages, trial terms, refunds, deployment target, and data-retention terms. Do not fabricate these or activate billing while implementing the seed.

## Effort buys additional work, not inflated claims

The authoritative method definitions are in [effort.md](skills/brainstorm/references/effort.md).

| Profile | Distinct contribution | Evidence of the contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Develop a useful opening from available context. | A specific lead, its mechanism, and material uncertainty. |
| Focused | Ground and develop the lead; examine a hinge and a meaningful rival or adverse case. | The actual material used, the assumption challenged, and what the comparison changed. |
| Deep | Investigate the critical dependency, compare competing explanations, and revise the candidate after a discriminating example or authorized probe. | Sources/artifacts actually inspected, alternatives distinguished, and revisions or remaining evidence gaps. |

The same short answer could result from any profile. More words, domains, agents, citations, or tokens do not establish greater effort. A requested Deep pass with unavailable evidence must report its limitation, not simulate research.

Adjustable dimensions are **grounding, causal depth, breadth, challenge, and artifact work**. “Deep on this one mechanism, no new branches, short answer” is coherent. These are method controls, not hidden provider settings or safety exemptions.

Keep honest uncertainty, source conservation, privacy, recovery, accessibility, and safe operation in every tier. Free must be a useful process, not a deliberately misleading answer whose missing safeguards require payment.

## What the paywall can and cannot enforce

The ordinary skills are public MIT-licensed material. Publishing them is compatible with offering a paid managed service, but a customer can also read and use the instructions in their own agent. Do not describe their standalone use as unauthorized or pretend the prompt text is a secret premium asset.

**Recommended paid boundary:** access to the product's managed Focused/Deep preparation and continuity workflows, plus any additional service work actually supplied. Make the paid value real: material gathering where supported, pinned context and methods, preserved decisions, reliable continuation, and honest effort/result records. Do not promise features before they exist.

A server that merely returns public Markdown has limited service differentiation. Do not market that as guaranteed deeper model reasoning. The user's chosen agent may do additional thinking independently; Made Well is not a workforce and cannot police cognition outside its managed operations.

Prompt instructions may describe allowed effort, but **they are not the enforcement mechanism**. The authenticated service must authorize the selected workflow before preparing a protected operation or incurring a billable effect. A button hidden in the UI, `paid: true` from a client/model, a skill name, an MCP client name, or a user-supplied “entitlement” document is not authority.

Keep source/skill access, access to the user's work, entitlement to a managed effort profile, and permission to perform external actions separate. Paid access never grants cross-tenant reads, broader file roots, deployment, extra agent spawning, or implementation permission.

## Recommended access behavior

| Situation | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| No effort selected | Select Light; never opt the user into paid work. |
| Light requested | Allow the free profile, subject to ordinary identity/work permissions and actual capability. |
| Focused/Deep requested with verified entitlement | Resolve and pin the selected profile; preserve the user's other constraints. |
| Focused/Deep requested without entitlement | Return an actionable access requirement and offer Light or the approved upgrade route; do not charge, silently downgrade, or falsely report paid work. |
| Entitlement cannot be verified | Do not grant a new protected operation. Preserve drafts/results and offer an available free path without pretending verification succeeded. |
| Host lacks necessary evidence/tool access | Disclose the capability gap independently of payment status. Payment does not make local files or unavailable tools readable. |
| Custom dials, aliases, nested methods, or a resume imply protected managed work | Validate the resolved composition against the service entitlement; a cosmetic `light` label must not bypass a paid operation. |
| A previously accepted operation is retried | Recover its stable logical receipt/result under work authorization; do not initiate a second purchase or count transport retries as new work. |
| Access changes during a conversation | Apply an explicit saved-boundary policy to new protected operations. Preserve authorized access to earlier user material; do not rewrite history or silently change the saved profile. |

The last row requires a documented entitlement-expiry/in-flight policy before a paid launch. Do not guess whether a long-running paid action is canceled, completed, or refunded.

Enforce declared operations and resolved recipes, not a model's subjective self-rating of how hard it thought. Normalize natural-language selections into the actual validated service contract before authorization. Public standalone skill use stays outside the service paywall.

## Usage and billing boundaries

Do not choose quotas from this document. “Free” does not settle unlimited hosted usage; “paid” does not settle a unit, allowance, or overage charge.

If metering is later selected:
- Define the customer-visible unit before implementing a counter. A conversational turn, preparation, completed investigation, and MCP network request are different things.
- Use stable business-operation identity across CLI/UI/MCP paths. A new transport request ID is not necessarily a new paid operation.
- Keep authoritative entitlement/payment facts separate from user/model claims and generated outputs.
- Do not bill catalog reads, previews, retries, errors, or partial work under an unstated policy.
- Preserve requested, authorized, effective, and actually performed effort separately. Client-reported work is attributed evidence, not automatically a verified fulfillment receipt.
- Handle concurrent requests and uncertain payment effects with the actual provider's supported idempotency/reconciliation contract. A local “charged” flag alone does not prove exactly-once effects.
- Never initiate a subscription, purchase, overage, or payment-provider account merely because the user selects Deep.

Hosted inference is not included by implication. Keep the existing provider-independent, user-chosen-tool boundary unless separately authorized product scope changes it.
